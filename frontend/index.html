Core Cybersecurity Principle

Strong Account Security ≠ Strong Privacy

A user may possess a 25-character password and hardware MFA token (High Security), yet publicly share their phone number, live venue locations, and family relationships (High Exposure / Low Privacy).

This framework bridges that gap by systematically quantifying behavioral exposure vectors that firewalls and passwords cannot protect.

Security

Protects systems, credentials, and encrypted communications from unauthorized access, breach, and brute force.

Privacy

Controls how personal identity, daily routines, social circles, and contact channels are shared and harvested.
