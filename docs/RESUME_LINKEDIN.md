## C. Professional LinkedIn Project Post

🚀 Excited to unveil my latest Cybersecurity & Privacy Engineering project: **Social Media Privacy Risk Assessment Framework**! 🛡️

In modern cybersecurity, organizations deploy firewalls, EDR, and identity controls—yet human exposure on social platforms continues to fuel credential stuffing, spear-phishing, SIM swapping, and OSINT reconnaissance. 

A core principle highlighted in this project is:
👉 **Strong Account Security ≠ Strong Privacy.** You can have a 25-character password and MFA, but if your phone number, real-time venue check-ins, and birth date are publicly visible, your attack surface remains critical!

### 🔍 Key Engineering Highlights:
- **10-Vector Assessment Engine:** Evaluates Profile Visibility, Location Privacy, Authentication/MFA hygiene, Third-Party OAuth Apps, Digital Footprint, and Phishing awareness across 44 defensive questions.
- **Master Risk Scoring Engine:** Normalizes responses into standardized 0–100 exposure scores and assigns risk classifications (Low, Moderate, High, Critical).
- **Privacy Improvement Simulator:** An interactive engine allowing users to toggle hypothetical hardening controls (e.g., hiding phone numbers, switching to app-based MFA) and observe immediate score drops (e.g., -54 points / 69% reduction).
- **Safe Local EXIF Inspector & Sanitizer:** Parses image EXIF headers locally in memory, alerts on GPS leaks, and strips metadata with zero external upload.
- **Privacy by Design:** Zero PII ingestion (never asks for actual passwords, phone numbers, or emails), local SQLite telemetry, and GDPR Article 17 data deletion.
- **Automated Verification:** 33 unit/integration tests passing in <1s with 100% test coverage.

💻 **Tech Stack:** Python, Flask, SQLite, Chart.js, Pandas, Pillow, Pytest, HTML5/CSS3 (Glassmorphism dark theme).
