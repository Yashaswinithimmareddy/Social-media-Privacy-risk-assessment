# Social Media Privacy & Hardening Checklist
**Defensive Cybersecurity Operational Guidance**

Use this printable checklist to systematically audit and harden your personal and organizational social media accounts.

---

### Phase 1: Core Account Security & Authentication
- [ ] **Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA / 2FA):** Configure an Authenticator App (Google Authenticator, Bitwarden, 1Password) or FIDO2 hardware security key. Avoid relying exclusively on SMS text verification.
- [ ] **Generate a Strong, Unique Passphrase:** Ensure the account password is at least 16 characters in length, complex, and never reused across other services. Store it in an encrypted password manager.
- [ ] **Turn On Unrecognized Login Alerts:** Configure platform notifications to immediately send email and push alerts whenever a login attempt originates from an unfamiliar device or browser.
- [ ] **Audit Active Logged-In Sessions:** Navigate to `Settings -> Security & Login -> Active Sessions`. Review all connected smartphones, laptops, and smart TVs; click **"Log Out of All Other Sessions"**.

---

### Phase 2: Personal & Contact Information Privacy
- [ ] **Hide Mobile Phone Number:** Set phone number visibility to **"Only Me"** or remove it entirely from public profile bios to prevent SIM-swapping and SMS smishing.
- [ ] **Privatize Personal Email Address:** Restrict your primary email address from public profile buttons or use an email forwarding alias.
- [ ] **Hide Birth Year:** Configure your date of birth to show only day and month, or hide the date completely to shield identity theft data elements.
- [ ] **Remove Granular Residential Locations:** Delete specific street addresses, apartment complexes, or neighborhood landmarks from public bio fields.
- [ ] **Decouple Public Family Linkages:** Remove explicit parent, sibling, or child tags that could reveal answers to account recovery security questions.

---

### Phase 3: Location Privacy & Anti-Surveillance
- [ ] **Disable Real-Time Live Location Sharing:** Turn off continuous location broadcasts and story geotags.
- [ ] **Practice Delayed Posting:** Never check in to restaurants, gyms, or hotels while physically present. Publish vacation albums only after returning home safely.
- [ ] **Disable Camera EXIF Geotagging:** Turn off GPS location tagging in your mobile camera settings to avoid embedding exact latitude/longitude in uploaded images.
- [ ] **Obfuscate Recurring Daily Routines:** Avoid posting fitness tracker maps with identifiable residential street names or predictable daily commute paths.

---

### Phase 4: Tagging, Mentions & Content Controls
- [ ] **Enable Tag Review / Timeline Approval:** Require explicit manual consent before tagged photos, videos, or check-ins appear on your profile.
- [ ] **Restrict Who Can Tag You:** Change tagging permissions from "Everyone" to "Friends Only" or "No One".
- [ ] **Opt Out of Automated Facial Recognition:** Disable automated facial suggestion and biometric tagging across photo uploads.
- [ ] **Inspect Backgrounds Before Posting:** Check photos for visible company badges, boarding pass barcodes, keys, or workstation computer screens.
- [ ] **Set Default Audience to Friends or Custom:** Change default future post visibility from "Public" to "Friends Only".

---

### Phase 5: Friends, Followers & Social Engineering Defense
- [ ] **Verify Unfamiliar Connection Requests:** Never accept connection or follow requests from unfamiliar profiles or accounts with no mutual connections.
- [ ] **Perform a Biannual Connection Audit:** Review friends/followers and remove inactive, duplicate, or unverified contacts.
- [ ] **Never Forward One-Time Passwords (OTPs):** Remember that friends, support agents, and colleagues will NEVER legitimately ask for your 6-digit verification code.
- [ ] **Treat Urgent DM Links as Hostile:** If a contact sends an unexpected message ("Is that you in this video?"), verify out-of-band before clicking.
- [ ] **Ignore Unsolicited Giveaways and Crypto Offers:** Immediately report and block accounts promoting unrequested prizes or investment collaborations.

---

### Phase 6: Third-Party Applications & Digital Footprint Hygiene
- [ ] **Audit Connected OAuth Integrations:** Navigate to `Settings -> Apps and Websites`. Revoke access for any games, quizzes, or tools unused in the last 90 days.
- [ ] **Limit App Permissions:** Enforce the Principle of Least Privilege by revoking inbox and post-publishing permissions from external applications.
- [ ] **Audit Historical Posts:** Use the platform's "Limit Past Posts" tool to mass-privatize public posts older than 3 years.
- [ ] **Delete Abandoned Accounts:** Identify dormant accounts on legacy platforms (Tumblr, old Twitter, MySpace) and permanently close them.
- [ ] **Request Your Data Archive:** Periodically download your platform data archive to inspect stored location telemetry and advertising profile segments.
