Prioritized Remediations

[IMMEDIATE] Remove or strictly hide your mobile phone number from all public profiles.
Navigate to Account Settings -> Personal Information -> Contact Info -> Set Phone Visibility to 'Only Me' or remove it from public display.
[IMMEDIATE] Hide your birth year or privatize your full date of birth completely.
Go to Edit Profile -> Basic Info -> Birthday -> Toggle Birth Year to 'Only Me' or hide the entire date.
[IMMEDIATE] Remove residential street address, apartment number, or specific neighborhood from your bio.
Edit Profile -> Places Lived -> Remove specific residential entries or set to general region only.
[IMMEDIATE] Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) immediately using a mobile authenticator app.
Go to Settings -> Security & Login -> Two-Factor Authentication -> Choose 'Authentication App' (e.g. Google Authenticator, Bitwarden, or 1Password) and save backup codes.
[IMMEDIATE] Disable real-time location sharing and avoid live venue updates.
Turn off device-level location access for the social media app in Phone Settings -> Privacy -> Location Services. Stop broadcasting live stories.
